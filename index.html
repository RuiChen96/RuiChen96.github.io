About Me

I am an Algorithm Expert in the DreamX Team at AMAP, Alibaba Group. I received my master’s degree from the Dept. of Computer Sci. & Tech. at Tsinghua University in 2021, supervised by Prof. Haizhou Ai.

My research interests include video world models, multimodal reasoning, visual generation and evaluation, and embodied intelligence.
